Put ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in this folder.

Download a Windows build from https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ or https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases
(unzip it and copy the two files from its "bin" folder here).

If they are missing here, Tunes Converter falls back to any ffmpeg/ffprobe found on your PATH.

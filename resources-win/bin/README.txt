Put ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe in this folder (directly inside it, not in a sub-folder).

Easiest: open Tunes Converter and press "Download ffmpeg" in the yellow box.
Or download a Windows build yourself from https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/ (open its "bin" folder and copy the two files here).

If they are missing here, Tunes Converter uses any ffmpeg/ffprobe found on your PATH.
